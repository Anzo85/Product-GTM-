> Module 3 · Master Product Positioning — ★ Deliverable 3
>
> Lock the positioning framework, then write and pressure-test the statement.

## 1. Positioning framework (six components)

| Component | Your answer |
|---|---|
| **Target customer** — who is it for? | Friend-group trip organizers planning leisure stays for 4–16 people. |
| **Market category** — what is it? | Group trip booking (plan, vote, book and pay together). |
| **Key benefit** — the #1 value | Book the place everyone wants without fronting everyone's money. |
| **Competitive alternative** — vs. what? | Booking on one card and collecting later through payment apps and reminders. |
| **Differentiator** — why us, uniquely | Dropout-proof split checkout: each person pays their share from a link, and the booking holds even when someone is late. |
| **Proof** — evidence it's true | Hold-and-re-split mechanics built into checkout; pilot share-completion rate (target ≥ 85%); documented demand from Airbnb's split-payment launch and survey. |

## 2. Core product truth

On Roamly Groups, **the organizer's card only ever pays the organizer's share — and the booking still stands if a friend flakes.** Competitors either make one person pay for everyone or cancel the whole booking when one person misses a deadline.

## 3. Positioning statement

> For **friend-group trip organizers** who **are tired of fronting thousands and chasing friends for money**, **Roamly Groups** is the **group trip booking experience** that **lets everyone vote on the stay and pay their own share at checkout**. Unlike **booking on one card and collecting later**, Roamly Groups **keeps the booking safe even when someone flakes — so the organizer is never the bank.**

**One-liner:** Book together. Pay separately. Nobody's the bank.

## 4. Pressure tests

| Test | Pass? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Ownable** — could a competitor say the same? | ⚠️ Partial | "Split payments" alone is copyable. What's ownable is the organizer framing plus "the booking never collapses." Lead with that, not with "split." |
| **Believable** — is the proof real? | ⚠️ Not yet | Demand proof is real; product proof needs pilot numbers. Replace targets with actual completion rate after the pilot. |
| **Relevant** — does the ICP care? | ✅ Yes | Fronting money and chasing friends is the organizer's most-cited pain. |
| **Durable** — holds as the product grows? | ✅ Yes | "Nobody's the bank" extends naturally to experiences, transport and shared trip costs later. |

## 5. Positioning vs. messaging note

The positioning stays fixed; the message adapts to the moment:

- **Homepage / app store:** lead with the outcome — *"Book together. Pay separately."*
- **In-app, on a 4+-guest search:** lead with the action — *"Traveling as 6? Everyone can pay their own share at checkout."*
- **Invite link (to invitees):** lead with speed and trust — *"Maya booked the Lisbon house. Your share is €214 — pay in a minute to lock your spot."*
