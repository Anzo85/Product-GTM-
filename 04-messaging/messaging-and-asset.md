# Messaging Pillars & AI-Generated Asset — Roamly Groups

> Module 4 · Deliver Messaging That Captivates Customers — ★ Deliverable 4
>
> Turn positioning into a message that moves people: set the market context, build a pillar per audience, activate it across surfaces, and produce one AI-generated asset.

## 1. From/To market shift

| | |
|---|---|
| **From** (life before) | One person books the trip, fronts the money, and spends weeks chasing everyone. Group trips are planned in chats and paid for on spreadsheets. |
| **To** (life after) | Group trips are decided and paid for together, at the moment of booking — the way people already split dinners and rides. Everyone commits with their own card. |

## 2. Audience segmentation

- **Number of audiences that need their own pillar:** 2
- **Justification:**
  - **The Organizer** (buyer and decider) wants control and to stop carrying financial risk. They choose the stay, start the Group and decide on upgrades.
  - **The Invitee** (payer and likely new user) never chose Roamly. They need to trust a link from a friend and pay in under a minute, with zero setup.
  - Same product, opposite jobs: one message can't do both. Hosts are a third stakeholder (they accept payment holds) but are reached through partner communications, not consumer messaging.

## 3. Messaging pillars

### Pillar — Audience 1: The Organizer

| Layer | Message |
|---|---|
| **🎯 Value prop** | Plan the trip, not the payback. Book the place everyone wants without lending anyone a cent. |
| **🏆 Capabilities** | Shared shortlist and voting; split at checkout (your card pays only your share); live "who's paid" tracker; automatic reminders; booking holds while friends pay; if someone is late, the gap is re-split or covered — the booking never collapses. |
| **📊 Evidence** | 43% of US travelers have lost $1,000+ to companions not paying (Airbnb/DKC survey). Pilot target: 85% of shares paid within 48 hours — _replace with actual pilot result and one organizer quote._ |

### Pillar — Audience 2: The Invitee

| Layer | Message |
|---|---|
| **🎯 Value prop** | Pay your share in a minute. No IOUs, no awkward reminders. |
| **🏆 Capabilities** | Pay from a link — no app or account needed first; Apple Pay, Google Pay or card; see exactly what you're paying for and the deadline; booking confirmation in your own name. |
| **📊 Evidence** | The link comes from a friend, so trust is borrowed. Pilot target: median time-to-pay under 5 minutes from opening the link — _replace with pilot data._ |

## 4. Activation across surfaces

Surfaces chosen for a product-led motion: where the organizer decides, where the invitee converts, and where a new organizer first hears of us.

| Surface | Copy variation |
|---|---|
| **In-app prompt** (search with 4+ guests) | "Traveling as 6? Everyone can pay their own share at checkout." |
| **Invite message** (the growth loop) | "Maya booked the Lisbon house 🏡 Your share is €214. Pay by Friday 6pm to lock your spot." |
| **App store / homepage headline** | "Book together. Pay separately. Nobody's the bank." |

## 5. AI-generated asset

- **Asset type:** Launch social ad (1080×1350, Instagram/TikTok feed format).
- **Prompt used:**
  > Create a 1080×1350 social ad as SVG for "Roamly Groups," a group-booking feature. Audience: friend-group trip organizers aged 26–38. Use only this approved copy: headline "Book together. Pay separately."; subline "Nobody's the bank."; product moment showing a Lisbon house for 6 friends, €214 per share, 5 of 6 paid, with the line "Kai is late? The booking still holds."; supporting line "Friends pay from a link. No app needed."; CTA "Start a Group". Dark navy #07162C background, blue accent #3B82F6, no photos, no stock imagery.
- **Output:** ![Launch asset](assets/launch-asset.svg)
- **What I edited and why:**
  - The first draft's subline was "Never pay for your friends again" — an overclaim (the organizer *can* still choose to cover a gap). Replaced with "Nobody's the bank."
  - The draft showed all 6 shares paid. Changed to 5 of 6 with one pending, because the unpaid share is the differentiator — the image must show the booking surviving a late friend.
  - Removed a "hassle-free" tagline: generic, and any competitor could claim it.

## 6. Blind-read result

Cold read done as an AI role-play (asset only, no context), to be repeated with a peer.

| Question | What they understood |
|---|---|
| Who is it built for? | "Someone booking a trip with a group of friends." ✅ |
| What does the product do? | "Lets each friend pay their part of the rental." ✅ — but "the booking still holds" was read as "Roamly pays for Kai." ⚠️ |
| What makes you trust it? | "The tracker looks real." Weak — no proof point on the asset. |

**Last edit from the gap:** added "Re-split the gap or cover it. Your call." under the late-payer line, so it's clear the group decides and Roamly isn't lending money.
